Delete a ChurchSnap account

This page provides the web pathway for permanently deleting a registered ChurchSnap account and associated personal account data.

Open ChurchSnap Web

Submit the deletion through ChurchSnap Web

  1. Open ChurchSnap Web using the button above.
  2. Sign in with the registered account you want to delete.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Choose Delete My Account.
  5. Review the data-removal and retention information.
  6. Enter the current password, type DELETE, and confirm.

This web process works without reinstalling the Android app.

Leadership accounts

Administrators, pastors, ministry leaders, and small-group leaders must transfer their protected responsibilities before deletion. This prevents a church from losing essential management access.

What is deleted

ChurchSnap deletes the Firebase sign-in account, active sessions, member or registered-visitor profile, protected private-profile information, profile picture, prayer requests, pending group or ministry requests, attendance check-ins, bookmarks, and personal schedule assignments associated with the account.

What may be retained

Confirmed giving records may be retained in anonymized form for church accounting. Security and administrator audit history, and encrypted backups created before deletion, may remain for legitimate recordkeeping, fraud prevention, security, or recovery. Deletion does not remove church-owned sermons, events, media, ministries, announcements, or other congregation-wide records.
